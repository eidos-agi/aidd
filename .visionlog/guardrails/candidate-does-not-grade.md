---
title: "candidate does not grade"
type: "guardrail"
date: "2026-08-23"
---

`product-vN` does not edit held-out evals, critic calibration, quality thresholds, or the validation panel in the same run that authors the candidate.

The builder does not approve its own UI. The builder does not grade its own deletions. A public eval the builder can see is not held-out.
